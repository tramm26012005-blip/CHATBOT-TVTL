# Healing audio credits

- recorded-rain.ogg: Rain.ogg, Wikimedia Commons, public domain. Original metadata and author credit are preserved in src/lib/healing-recordings.json. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain.ogg
- recorded-ocean.ogg: Ocean Waves on a Tropical Beach.ogg, Jarrod stanley / J.D. Savanyu, CC0. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ocean_Waves_on_a_Tropical_Beach.ogg
- *-v2.wav: procedural arrangements and synthesized soundscapes generated locally by frontend/scripts/generate-ambient.py. These are synthesized audio, not field recordings. Each music arrangement uses an independent melody, chord order and tempo; focus/upbeat arrangements include percussion and bass.

Run the generator with Python and numpy. It preserves entries in healing-recordings.json; do not remove the two recorded OGG assets when rebuilding.
